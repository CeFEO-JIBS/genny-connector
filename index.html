Ask Genny about Generation 6 knowledge

Genny connects Claude to everything Generation6 has published — the blog and the Academy curriculum — so you can find what we've already said before you write it again.

It is most useful in the middle of client work. Have we written about this? What do we teach on it? Who said it, and where can I point them? Those questions have answers, but they were scattered across six years of posts and a video curriculum nobody re-reads.

How to add it →

What it can answer

  • What does the Academy teach about triangulation?
  • What have we published on employing family members?
  • Which frameworks do we teach without crediting a source?
  • Find everything we've said about dividends and payout expectations.

What it is not

Genny knows only what Generation6 has published: 59 blog posts and 39 Academy scripts. It does not know your clients, your engagements, your notes, or anything said in a workshop. It has no access to the wider research literature.

If it can't answer something, that usually means we haven't written about it — which is worth knowing in itself.

Who can use it

Anyone with a @generation6.com address. Other addresses can be added individually — ask Massimo. Everything is per-person: your access can be granted and withdrawn without affecting anyone else.
