The blog

Every post published on generation6.com from 2021 to today, by the founders and the advisor network. Twenty-one of them first appeared as FamilyBusiness.org columns; Genny includes them and tells you where they were originally published, so you can cite the original.

The Academy

The full script of the taught curriculum — five tracks across twelve units. Genny can show you the curriculum as a structure, not just search it, which is useful when the question is about coverage rather than a topic.

It also knows the 145 concepts the Academy teaches and the attribution each script actually gives. Sixteen carry a named source; the rest don't. That distinction is recorded honestly: an unattributed concept means the script credited no one, not that the idea is ours.

What isn't in it

Client work, engagement notes, workshop material, the director bank, and anything unpublished. The wider family-business research literature is not here either — that's a different tool.

How new material arrives

New posts and scripts are added deliberately, not automatically. If you've published something recently and Genny doesn't know it, that's expected rather than broken — say so and it gets added.
