Counts verified 30 July 2026.

The blog

59 posts published on generation6.com since 2021, by the founders and the advisor network. Twenty-one first appeared as FamilyBusiness.org columns — Genny includes those and tells you where each was originally published, so you can cite the original.

The Academy

The full script of the taught curriculum: 39 scripts, five tracks, twelve units. Genny can show you the curriculum as a structure rather than only searching it, which is what you want when the question is about coverage rather than a topic.

The books

Six books written or edited by Generation6 advisors — 78 chapters, 260,000 words, published between 2008 and 2024, on branding, cohesion, professionalization, and next-generation leadership. Genny searches them chapter by chapter and can return the full text.

What we teach, and who we credit

Genny has indexed 1,106 named concepts and frameworks — 145 from the Academy, 961 from the books. Seventy credit a named source; the rest don't. That gap is recorded rather than tidied away: no attribution means the script or the book credited no one, not that the idea started with us. Which frameworks do we teach without crediting anyone is a question nothing else can answer.

What isn't in it

Client work, engagement notes, workshop material, the director talent bank, and anything unpublished. The wider family-business research literature isn't here either — that's a different tool.

How new material arrives

Posts, scripts and books are added deliberately, not automatically. Blog posts arrive in batches; books arrive rarely. If we've published something recently and Genny doesn't know it, that's expected rather than broken — say so and it gets added.
